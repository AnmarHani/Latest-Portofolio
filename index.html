About Me

Over three years in this field. AI changed all of it, sure — but I got here before that, back when the job meant digging through research, making do with what few resources existed, and writing every line by hand. Most of my experience is Python and JavaScript, though I've worked with plenty else. I've moved through more than a few areas and kept trying to make them talk to each other: web, DevOps, cloud, security, and the rest. AI too, obviously. And then the ones that were supposed to be legendary — no idea what happened to those. Blockchain, for one. I was in there too.
